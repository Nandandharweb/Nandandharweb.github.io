Biography

I am a first-year Doctoral Scholar (Ph.D.) in the Economics & Social Sciences Area at the Indian Institute of Management Visakhapatnam (IIMV), currently completing doctoral coursework. My academic training focuses on econometrics, quantitative analysis, and empirical public policy.

Prior to joining IIM Visakhapatnam, I earned my M.A. in Economics from Tripura Central University (Academic Rank 1, CGPA 8.85) and worked as a Research Assistant on an ICSSR-sponsored project examining socio-economic policy and traditional ecological knowledge in Tripura. I have also completed research and statistical internships with the National Statistical Office (NSO), IIT Guwahati, and the International Institute of SDGs and Public Policy Research (IISPPR).

Digital Public Infrastructure and Credit Allocation in Fragmented Rural Economies

Nandan Dhar, Co-author Name (Advisor) • Manuscript available upon request (2026)

This paper exploits the phased regional rollout of unified open banking APIs to evaluate credit delivery across unbanked rural districts. Using a stacked difference-in-differences estimator, we find a 28% increase in formal credit uptake among micro-enterprises with reduced default rates through verifiable payment trails.

Network Friction and Merchant Adoption in Real-Time Mobile Payment Systems

Nandan Dhar, Senior Collaborator • Revise & Resubmit (2025)

We investigate two-sided network externalities during the nationwide expansion of digital QR payments. Analyzing high-frequency transaction logs across urban retail clusters, we document critical tipping points in vendor participation driven by consumer switching costs.

Nandan Dhar • Working Paper (2025)
