Biography

I am a Doctoral Researcher (PhD Candidate) in the Economics & Social Sciences Area at the Indian Institute of Management Visakhapatnam (IIMV). My primary research interests lie at the intersection of Applied Microeconomics, Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), Econometric Methods, and Technology Policy.

My dissertation examines how technological innovations—such as unified digital payment architectures, high-frequency transactions, and algorithmic market platforms—influence consumer welfare, financial inclusion, and market resilience across developing economies. Prior to entering the doctoral program at IIM Visakhapatnam, I received my academic training in Economics from premier institutions in India.

I utilize both structural and reduced-form empirical microeconometric strategies (e.g., difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, and instrumental variables) alongside administrative dataset pipelines to evaluate policies and real-world technology deployments.
